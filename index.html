moviii

Track the movies and TV you watch, privately on your phone.

What it does

  • Log what you've watched, with the date
  • Keep a watchlist of what's next
  • Follow your progress through TV shows, episode by episode
  • Get recommendations based on what you've watched
  • Optionally sync with your Trakt account
