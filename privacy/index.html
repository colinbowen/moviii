Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version

moviii keeps your movie and TV library on your phone. There's no moviii account, no moviii server, no ads, and no analytics. The app contacts The Movie Database (TMDB) to fetch titles and posters. If you choose to connect a Trakt account, it also syncs your library with Trakt.

What's stored on your phone

Everything you add to moviii is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Movies and episodes you've marked as watched, with the dates you watched them
  • Your watchlist and your progress through TV shows
  • Your app settings

If you connect Trakt, your Trakt sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure keychain. We (the developer) can't see any of this data.

Services the app connects to

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
The Movie Database (TMDB)Whenever you browse or searchYour search words and which titles you open, plus your IP address as part of any web request. It doesn't receive your library or any account details.
TraktOnly if you connect a Trakt accountYour watch history and watchlist, which are synced both ways. Trakt handles that data under its own privacy policy.
Apple App StoreOccasionally, after you've logged a few titlesA request to show Apple's built-in rating prompt. Apple decides whether to show it, and moviii never sees your rating.

moviii doesn't send your data anywhere else.

Importing from other apps

You can import your history from a Letterboxd or TV Time export file. moviii reads the file on your phone and never uploads it. To match titles, it looks them up on TMDB by name or ID, as it does when you search.

What moviii doesn't do

  • No moviii account and no sign-up
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of your data

The developer of moviii never receives any of your data.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Trakt from the Profile screen at any time. moviii deletes its Trakt sign-in from your phone and stops syncing. Anything already on Trakt stays there, and you can delete it on trakt.tv.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling moviii. That removes your library and settings from your phone. We hold no copy, so there's nothing to request from us.

Children

moviii isn't aimed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If moviii starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version of the app is released, and the date at the top will change.
